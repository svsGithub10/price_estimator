How starter prices were chosen

Prices were collected from publicly listed Indian supplier pages searched on 29 October 2026. These pages do not all describe the same grade or region, so they are reference starting points only. The app labels rates as “Listed” when a close thickness/variant and price were found, and “Estimated” when a rate was interpolated or a directly matching listing was unavailable.

  • Thermocol rates are calculated from example piece prices and sizes on supplier listings; the listed sheet sizes differ, so use your own stock dimensions and purchase cost.
  • MDF values use online price lists for 2 mm, 3 mm, 5 mm and nearby thicknesses; 6 mm is estimated between nearby listings.
  • PVC foam values use available sheet listings and economy-grade price lists. Density and quality can produce very different prices.
  • PVC/WPC board uses listed WPC values for 6 mm, 8 mm and 12 mm; 3 mm is estimated because a matching listing was not found.
  • ACP rates are starter references for 3 mm PE signage/interior grades. Premium finishes are set higher based on published finish ranges.
  • Acrylic rates use online 8×4 ft price lists. 4 mm and some special acrylic variants are estimated from nearby thickness values.
  • Adhesive mirror acrylic is based on a nearby self-adhesive mirror acrylic listing; an exact 2 mm listing was not found.
